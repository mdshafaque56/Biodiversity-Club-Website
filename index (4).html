How it ran

Planning and ideation

Rules, themes, submission guidelines and file naming, with a timeline and evaluation criteria.

Publicity and marketing

Posters, teasers and story updates, with calls for entry across campus groups, club handles and mailing lists.

Submissions and data management

Entries collected through the official portal, then sorted into secure cloud folders.

Evaluation and results

Anonymised entries scored on composition, creativity and theme relevance. Winners announced on social media.
